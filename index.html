FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can join?

Homegrown Indian brands with a real product, and Instagram creators aged 18 or older with a public account.

Do I need a big following?

No. Brands often look for smaller creators with engaged, loyal audiences. What matters most is fit.

How does a product exchange work?

A brand lists a product. You apply, the brand picks you and ships it to your door, and you post an honest review within 14 days, labelled #gifted or #ad. You keep the product.

Do I have to give a positive review?

Never. Creators share their honest opinion. A free product never buys a good review.

Who pays for shipping?

The brand ships the product directly to the creator. PromoConnect does not sell or ship products.

How do payments work?

Brands in India pay by UPI, card, netbanking or wallet through Razorpay. Brands abroad can use PayPal. Creators get paid to their bank account or UPI ID. We never see or store your card or UPI details.
