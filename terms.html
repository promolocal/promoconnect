Terms of Use

Last updated: 1 October 2026

PromoConnect connects homegrown brands with social media creators for product collaborations. PromoConnect is run by Divyanshu Goyal in India ("we", "us"). By signing up, paying for a plan or taking part in a collaboration, you agree to these terms.

1. Who can join

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • The information you give us must be true and your own. Brands must have the right to act for the brand they register.

2. How collaborations work

  • We suggest matches between brands and creators. Nobody has to accept a match.
  • When both sides agree, the brand sends the product to the creator. In a gifted collaboration the creator keeps the product.
  • Our current prices are on the Pricing page. You will always see the price before you pay, and we will tell you before any price changes apply to you.

3. Payments

  • Payments are processed by Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking and wallets) and, for brands outside India, by PayPal. We do not store card, UPI or bank login details.
  • Plans renew monthly until cancelled. Refunds and cancellations follow our Refund and Cancellation Policy.
  • For paid deals, the brand's payment is held until the creator's post is checked, then released to the creator minus the fees shown on the Pricing page.

4. Creators agree to

  • Post the agreed content within 14 days of receiving the product, unless the brand agrees to more time.
  • Give honest opinions. A free product never requires a positive review.
  • Clearly label every collaboration post, for example #gifted, #ad or #collab, as Indian advertising rules require (ASCI guidelines and consumer protection law).
  • Not use fake followers, bought engagement or misleading claims, and keep the post up for at least 30 days.

5. Brands agree to

  • Send only safe, lawful products they have the right to sell, and only make product claims that are true.
  • Not ask creators for fake reviews, hidden advertising or medical and results claims that can't be supported.
  • Handle their own shipping, product returns and customer queries.
  • Follow tax rules that apply to them. For example, brands may need to deduct TDS when products given to one creator in a year exceed the legal limit. Brands should check this with their accountant.

6. Content ownership

  • Creators own the content they make. Brands may reshare a creator's post with credit, unless they agree otherwise in writing. Using content in paid ads needs the creator's separate permission.
  • The PromoConnect name, logo, designs, posts and materials belong to us. Please don't copy or use them without written permission.

7. Our role and limits

  • We introduce brands and creators. We are not the seller of any product and are not responsible for product quality, safety, delivery or results.
  • We don't guarantee any number of matches, sales, followers or posts.
  • To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect losses arising from a collaboration.

8. Removing accounts

We may refuse or remove any creator or brand who breaks these terms, gives false information or treats others unfairly. You can leave at any time by emailing us.

9. Changes and law

We may update these terms; the date at the top will change, and we will tell active members about important changes. These terms follow the laws of India.
