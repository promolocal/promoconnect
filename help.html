Common questions

Who can join as a creator?

Anyone aged 18 or older with a public Instagram account. Fill in the creator form and we review your profile, usually within 2 working days.

Do I need a lot of followers?

No. Brands often prefer smaller creators with an engaged, loyal audience. What matters most is that your content fits the product.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining is free for creators, and you keep every product you review. The paid plans, Pro (₹399 a month) and Elite (₹999 a month), are optional and give more applications.

How do I get a product?

When a brand fits your profile, we message you with the product and what to post. If you say yes, the brand ships it to your door at no cost to you.

Do I have to give a positive review?

Never. Share your honest opinion. A free product does not buy a good review, and brands agree to this when they join.

How long do I have to post?

14 days from the day the product arrives, unless you and the brand agree on more time. The post should stay up for at least 30 days.

How should I label the post?

Add #ad where people can see it, at the start of the caption or on the video itself. India's ASCI rules require this for free products too.

What does PromoConnect cost a brand?

Founding brands get their first collaborations free. After that, plans start at ₹999 a month. See the Pricing page for every plan.

How do I choose creators?

We send you creator profiles that match your product, with their followers, engagement, topics and city. You approve the ones you like, and only then ship.

Who ships the product?

The brand ships directly to the creator and shares the tracking number. PromoConnect does not sell or ship products.

What if a creator doesn't post?

We follow up first. If there is still no post 14 days after delivery, any cash paid for the deal is refunded, and the creator loses points and can be removed.

Can I reuse a creator's content?

You can reshare the post with credit. Using it in paid ads needs the creator's permission, which is included in the Elite plan.

How can I pay?

By UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), card, netbanking or wallet through Razorpay. Brands outside India can pay with PayPal.

When do creators get paid for paid deals?

The brand's payment is held until the post is checked. It is then sent to the creator's bank account or UPI ID, minus the fee shown on the Pricing page.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in the cases listed in our Refund and Cancellation Policy, such as a first plan payment within 7 days or a deal where no post was made.

How do you check creators and brands?

We look at a creator's real followers and engagement, and flag sudden follower spikes. Brands must have a real product page before their first listing.

How do I report a problem?

Email promoconnect.in@gmail.com with what happened. We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within 30 days.

What do you do with my details?

We use them to match you and arrange collaborations. We never sell your data. A delivery address goes only to a brand you agreed to work with. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I open My dashboard?

Go to My dashboard, type the email you joined with, and we email you a 6-digit code. Enter the code and you are in. There is no password to remember.

How do points work?

You earn 100 points for every completed collaboration and 50 bonus points for every post made on time. We update points after each collaboration is checked.

How do I get on the Top 10?

Complete collaborations to earn points, and switch on 'Show me on the Top 10' in your dashboard. Only creators who switch it on are shown.

Can I hide myself from the Top 10?

Yes. Switch off 'Show me on the Top 10' in your dashboard and your name is removed straight away.
