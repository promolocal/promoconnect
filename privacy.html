Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data PromoConnect collects, why, and your choices, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Creators: name, email, Instagram handle, follower range, content topics, city, WhatsApp number (optional), a link to your post, and a delivery address if you accept a collaboration. For cash payouts: PAN and bank details, collected and verified by our payment partner Razorpay.
  • Brands: contact name, email, WhatsApp number, brand name, Instagram handle, website, product details and billing details.

Why we use it

  • To match brands with suitable creators and arrange collaborations.
  • To process payments, payouts, refunds and required tax records.
  • To contact you about matches, collaborations and important updates.
  • To sign you in to My dashboard with a one-time code sent to your email, and to show your points and badges there.
  • To check that collaboration posts went live and are labelled correctly.

We don't sell your data, and we don't send unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

  • Brands considering a match see a creator's name, Instagram handle, follower range, topics, city and post link.
  • A creator's delivery address and phone number go only to the brand they've agreed to work with.
  • Matched creators see the brand's name, product and contact details.
  • The public Top 10 shows a creator's Instagram handle, city, topic, points and badges, only if the creator switches this on. You can switch it off any time in My dashboard.
  • Service providers that store or process data for us: Google (Forms, Sheets, Gmail), Razorpay and PayPal (payments). Razorpay and PayPal handle card, UPI and bank details under their own privacy policies; we never store them.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you're part of PromoConnect. If you ask us to delete it, we do so within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as payment and tax records.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing promoconnect.in@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent means we can no longer match you.
