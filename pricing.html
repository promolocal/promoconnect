How paid deals work

When a brand offers cash as well as a product, the brand pays the cash plus its commission when it accepts a creator. The money is held until the creator's post is checked, then paid to the creator minus their fee. If no post arrives within 14 days, the brand gets the cash back. See our refund policy.

Plans renew monthly and you can cancel any time. Yearly option: pay for 10 months and get 12.
