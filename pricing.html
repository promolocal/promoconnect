How payments work

Brands pay for their plan at the start of each month. Creators are paid for each video after we check that the post is live and labelled #ad. If a creator doesn't post within 14 days, the brand gets the money for that video back. See our refund policy.

Plans renew monthly and you can cancel any time. Yearly option: save about 17% when you pay for a year upfront.

Ways to pay: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), card, netbanking or wallet through Razorpay. Brands and creators outside India can pay with PayPal. Taxes extra where applicable.
