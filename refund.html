Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

PromoConnect sells online services: brand plans, creator plans, featured listings and managed campaigns. We do not sell or ship physical products.

Cancelling a plan

  • You can cancel a monthly plan (brand or Creator Pro) any time by emailing promoconnect.in@gmail.com.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the month you've paid for.

Refunds

  • First plan payment: full refund if you ask within 7 days of your first payment and no creator has been accepted on your account yet.
  • Renewals: monthly renewals are not refunded once the new month has started, but you can cancel so you aren't charged again.
  • Paid deals: if a creator does not post within 14 days of receiving the product, the brand's deal payment is refunded in full. If a dispute is decided in the brand's favour, the deal payment is refunded.
  • Featured listings: not refundable once the listing has gone live.
  • Managed campaigns: full refund if cancelled before we contact any creator; after that, refund of the unused part.
  • Charged by mistake or twice: always refunded in full.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method (UPI, card, bank or PayPal) within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Products and shipping

Brands ship products directly to creators, so product quality, delivery and returns are the brand's responsibility. Our services are delivered online: plans are active as soon as payment is confirmed.

How to ask

Email promoconnect.in@gmail.com with your name, the payment date and the reason. We reply within 48 hours.
